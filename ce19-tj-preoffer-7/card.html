Before the offer letter
The hiring fit confidence checklist
Ability: can they do the actual job today, not the one on the resume
Culture fit: their values match beliefs you have actually defined
Team dynamic: they fit the people who will sit right next to them
Shared motivation: the win is real for both sides
Capacity for growth: room to become what the role will need
Leadership alignment: how they want to be managed matches how you manage
Your own support systems: you have the time and tools to onboard them
Most bad hires are not misalignment. They are misalignment on one of these seven, and number seven is about you.
